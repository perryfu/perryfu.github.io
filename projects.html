Bidirectional switches: from devices to converters

2024–

A bidirectional switch blocks voltage and conducts current in both directions in a single device, replacing back-to-back transistor pairs in matrix and current-source converters, T-type converters, solid-state circuit breakers and solid-state transformers. This program characterizes these switches and puts them to work: monolithic versus discrete 3.3-kV SiC bidirectional FETs (switching loss with uncertainty quantification, transient modeling, and comparison with unidirectional MOSFETs), evaluation of 650-V bidirectional GaN devices, and bidirectional GaN converters for energy storage.

With A. Agarwal, W. Sung, A. J. Morgan, S. A. Mancini, S. Atcitty.

Wide- and ultra-wide-bandgap power devices: characterization, ruggedness and gate drive

2022–

From a new device to a reliable converter. For SiC: short-circuit ruggedness of state-of-the-art 3.3-kV MOSFETs; partial discharge in 3.3-kV power modules at flight-altitude pressures; insulation of a 15-kV module at 10 kV and >300 V/ns with a nonlinear field-grading coating; and a 99.977%-efficient, 20-kV, 50-A T-type modular DC circuit breaker built from 3.3-kV modules. For ultra-wide-bandgap β-Ga₂O₃: a power-over-fiber gate drive and the switching behavior of a 1.9-kV vertical FinFET, and a review of Ga₂O₃ transistors and diodes from the switching-performance perspective.

Insulation and partial discharge for electrified aircraft

2021–

How fast-switching SiC converters stress insulation at flight-altitude pressures: partial-discharge inception and aging of aviation wires, motor windings and PCBs under high-dv/dt PWM voltage, the effect of mission profile and temperature, and PCB flashover at varied air pressure — with physics-informed neural networks to estimate insulation power loss.

Isolated DC/DC conversion between low-voltage storage and medium-voltage grids: a hybrid multilevel high-gain converter, a bidirectional GaN semiconductor-galvanic-isolation converter, and a quasi-switched-capacitor bidirectional converter.

Completed

Power conversion and DC distribution on the lunar surface

2023–

Kilovolt-class power transmission for the Moon: 1.5-kV, 1.5-kW high-gain isolated DC/DC converters based on modular multilevel topologies, multi-winding transformers and their thermal design, a 120-V, 10-kW GaN-based flexible DC energy router, and stability and power-flow control of lunar DC microgrids. The team placed among the winners of NASA’s Watts on the Moon Challenge (Phase 2, Level 1 in 2022 and Level 2 in 2023), and the GaN energy router paper received the IEEE PELS Best ECCE Oral Presentation Paper on Emerging Technology Award (2023).
