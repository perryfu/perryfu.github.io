Biography and CV

Short bio

Pengyu (Perry) Fu is currently a research scientist at the Center for High Performance Power Electronics, The Ohio State University, working at the intersection of power, AI, and aerospace & space. His research covers high-voltage engineering; medium-voltage SiC devices and bidirectional switches; power conversion for electrified aircraft, lunar surface power; and physics-informed machine learning for power engineering. His work received the First Place Prize Paper Award of the IEEE Journal of Emerging and Selected Topics in Power Electronics (2026). He received his Ph.D. in electrical engineering from North China Electric Power University in 2019 and is a Senior Member of IEEE.
